Pedro Mejia-Alvarez

Professor and Researcher in Artificial Intelligence, Real-Time Systems, and Software Engineering

Pedro Mejia-Alvarez

I am a professor, researcher, and engineer at CINVESTAV whose work spans real-time systems, artificial intelligence, software engineering, embedded systems, dependable computing, and data-intensive systems. My research combines theoretical analysis with the design and implementation of software and system prototypes used to evaluate scheduling, operating-system, testing, debugging, and reliability techniques.

I earned a degree in Computer Systems Engineering from Tecnologico de Monterrey, Campus Queretaro, and a PhD in Computer Science/Informatics from the Universidad Politecnica de Madrid, where my doctoral specialization included Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. I later completed postdoctoral research in Computer Science at the University of Pittsburgh with Prof. Daniel Mosse.

My academic work includes research on real-time scheduling and fault tolerance, power- and energy-aware real-time computing, interrupt handling and operating systems, software engineering and testing, machine learning applications, real-time databases, embedded systems, and cybersecurity for real-time and cyber-physical systems. I have also contributed to the development of research and engineering software supporting these areas.
