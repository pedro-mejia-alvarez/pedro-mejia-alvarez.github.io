Teaching

My teaching record spans graduate courses in real-time systems, operating systems, software engineering, software testing and reliability, and related advanced topics. The detailed history below is translated from my 2025 curriculum vitae.

Main course areas

Real-Time Systems

Scheduling, timing analysis, real-time kernels, predictability, and applications of time-constrained computing.

Operating Systems

Operating-system concepts, processes, concurrency, scheduling, memory, and systems implementation.

Software Engineering

Software engineering methods, design, development processes, and advanced software-engineering topics.

Software Testing and Reliability

Testing techniques, software reliability, dependable software, and quality evaluation.

Advanced and Selected Topics

Advanced software engineering, selected mathematics topics, and specialized computing subjects.

Computing Foundations

Graduate computing foundations represented in the CV by Computing I.

Social and Professional Context of Computing

Social and professional dimensions of computing; taught as a shared course.
